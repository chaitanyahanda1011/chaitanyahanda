What documents are needed for a death cert.?▾
The MCCD, 2 passport photos, proof of residence, and applicant Aadhaar are typically required. Requirements vary by state.
How do I handle an NRI family case?▾
Assign a local field agent for physical visits. Use the remote document portal and schedule video check-ins.
How do I escalate a complex legal case?▾
Use the escalation button in the client profile to notify your supervisor and request a legal specialist.
